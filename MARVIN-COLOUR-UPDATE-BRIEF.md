## Confirmed Colour System (Brand Bible V6)

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Blue | `#004AAD` | Buttons, headers, strong CTAs |
| Accent Teal | `#34D1BC` | Accent text, links, hover states, badges |
| Dark Charcoal | `#1B1B1F` | Page background |
| Mid Charcoal | `#252529` | Card/surface background |
| Light Charcoal | `#2e2e34` | Nested card background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Body text on dark backgrounds |

---

## Notes
- Do NOT use any other blue variants (#2C75E4, #0057B8, #1A73E8, etc.) as accent colours.
- The Primary Blue (#004AAD) is used for buttons and strong CTAs only.
- The Accent Teal (#34D1BC) is the secondary/accent colour for all decorative and highlight elements.
- This is now locked in Brand Bible V6 (May 2026) and must be applied consistently across all Reviewly digital assets.
