The shift for Australian contractors

5 signs your trades business is invisible to AI

  • ChatGPT or Google's AI Overview names competitors but not you when asked for a local contractor.
  • Your business details, such as past addresses or old trading names, differ across directories and your site.
  • Your credentials, Master Builders memberships, and licenses are not stated in a machine-readable format.
  • Your reviews live entirely on one platform and are not corroborated across the broader web.
  • You rank on Google for specific keywords, but never appear in the AI answers above the standard results.

Why AI overlooks established trades businesses

AI cannot recommend what it does not trust. Many Australian trades businesses have a formidable offline reputation but scattered, inconsistent signals online. To an AI system evaluating options for a high-value contract, that ambiguity reads as risk — so it recommends a competitor with clearer entity signals instead.
