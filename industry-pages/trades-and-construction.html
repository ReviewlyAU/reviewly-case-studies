The shift for Australian contractors

Your customers now start with an AI assistant, not a page of search results. Homeowners, project managers, and commercial developers ask for a recommendation and act on the answer. The question is no longer "do we rank?" It is "does AI trust us enough to recommend us?"

5 signs your trades business is invisible to AI

  • ChatGPT or Google's AI Overview names competitors but not you when asked for a local contractor.
  • AI describes one of your locations well and another not at all, because your sites aren't represented consistently.
  • Your credentials, Master Builders memberships, and licences are not stated in a machine-readable format.
  • Your customer feedback appears in one source and lacks corroboration across the broader web.
  • You rank on Google for specific keywords, but never appear in the AI answers above the standard results.

Why AI overlooks established trades businesses

AI systems need clear evidence before they recommend a business. Many Australian trades businesses have a strong offline reputation but scattered, inconsistent signals online. When an AI system evaluates options for a high-value contract, that ambiguity weakens confidence and gives clearer competitors an advantage.

How Reviewly builds your visibility

We are a Visibility Architecture Partner. We turn scattered signals into structured authority using the REVIEW Method®. These are the five signals AI weighs before recommending a business:

  • Recognised: understood as a distinct, real entity with a defined service area.

How we'd approach a multi-site trades business

Every multi-site trades business is different, but the pattern is consistent. We start by checking how AI describes each of your locations today. Usually, it's inconsistent from one site to the next. We build one clear entity for the business and a verified entity for each site, so your licences, memberships, service areas and reviews line up everywhere AI looks.

Then we monitor how the major AI platforms describe and recommend each location over time. The goal is simple: when someone asks an AI assistant for a trusted contractor in any area you serve, the right site of your business is a name it can confidently give.
