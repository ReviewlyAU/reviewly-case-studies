## INTRO BLOCK
As search transitions from keyword-based indexing to AI-generated answers, the requirements for digital visibility have fundamentally changed. Traditional SEO metrics such as domain authority, backlink volume, and keyword density are no longer sufficient to guarantee that a brand will be cited or recommended by systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews.

This shift has led to the emergence of Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) agencies. However, because the discipline is new, there is currently no recognised independent ranking authority or standardised framework for evaluating these providers in Australia.

To address this gap, Reviewly has developed a transparent, objective methodology for assessing and categorising GEO agencies. This framework is used to compile our annual Best GEO Agencies in Australia guide (link to: /best-geo-agencies-australia/). Every agency listed in that guide has been evaluated against the five criteria below before inclusion.

## H2: The Five Assessment Criteria
**Body:** Each agency is evaluated across five independently verifiable criteria. An agency must satisfy all five to be included in the guide. There is no weighting system; a failure on any single criterion results in exclusion regardless of performance on the others.

### Criterion 1 of 5: Verified Service Offering
The agency must demonstrably offer dedicated GEO or AEO services as a named, active service line. This is verified by direct review of the agency's current website, service pages, and publicly available materials. Agencies that mention GEO only in passing blog content, or that reframe existing SEO services as GEO without a distinct methodology, are excluded. The service must be something a client can actively purchase and receive.

### Criterion 2 of 5: Published Methodology
The agency must have a documented, publicly accessible methodology that explains how they approach GEO or AEO. This does not need to be a detailed technical whitepaper, but it must go beyond generic marketing language. Reviewly looks for evidence of a structured process: how the agency audits current AI visibility, how they identify citation gaps, and how they implement improvements. Agencies with no published methodology are excluded.

### Criterion 3 of 5: Demonstrated Results
The agency must have at least one publicly available case study, client testimonial, or verifiable outcome that demonstrates a measurable improvement in AI citation visibility, share of voice in generative search, or a related metric. Reviewly does not accept self-reported metrics without supporting evidence. The result must be attributable to the agency's GEO or AEO work specifically, not to general digital marketing activity.

### Criterion 4 of 5: Industry Specialisation
GEO is not a one-size-fits-all discipline. The signals required to make a local healthcare clinic visible to AI engines are fundamentally different from those required for an enterprise SaaS brand or an eCommerce retailer. Reviewly evaluates whether the agency has a clear, stated industry focus or whether they have demonstrated specialised expertise in a particular sector. Generalist agencies that claim to serve all industries equally are assessed more critically on their methodology and results.

### Criterion 5 of 5: Technical Capability
Effective GEO requires a minimum level of technical capability: the ability to implement structured data (schema markup), manage entity disambiguation, and understand how AI crawlers and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems consume and process web content. Reviewly assesses whether the agency has demonstrable technical skills in these areas, either through their published content, their team credentials, or their documented service delivery process.

## H2: Scoring and Categorisation
**Body:** Agencies that pass all five criteria are then categorised by their primary industry specialisation and methodological strength. The categorisation is not a ranking of overall quality; it is a guide to help businesses identify the agency most aligned with their specific sector and buying journey.

**Table: Agency Categorisation Framework**

| Category | Primary Focus | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|---|
| **Service Businesses & Local Trades** | Local entity trust, reputation architecture | Proximity signals, verifiable entity corroboration |
| **B2B SaaS & Enterprise Pipeline** | Vendor comparison, feature extraction | Integration with CRM and lead generation |
| **eCommerce & Product Discovery** | Product catalogue structure, technical schema | Ability to handle massive databases for AI retrieval |
| **Enterprise Digital Transformation** | DXP integration, complex headless CMS | Alignment with multi-year corporate roadmaps |
| **SEO-to-AEO Content Migration** | Restructuring existing content equity | Frameworks for transitioning traditional SEO to AI answers |
| **Technical & Global GEO** | RAG architecture, international entity strategy | Deep technical crawl logic and E-E-A-T signals |

## H2: The Submission Process
**Body:** Reviewly actively monitors the Australian digital marketing landscape to identify emerging GEO and AEO providers. However, agencies are also welcome to submit themselves for evaluation.

To be considered for the next update of the Best GEO Agencies in Australia guide, agencies must provide:
1. Links to their active GEO/AEO service pages.
2. Documentation of their specific methodology.
3. At least one verifiable case study demonstrating improved AI visibility.

Submissions are reviewed by Reviewly's internal strategy team. There is no cost associated with submission or inclusion in the guide. Reviewly maintains strict editorial independence and does not accept paid placements or sponsorships for the guide.

## FAQ SECTION
**H2:** Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How does Reviewly assess GEO agencies?**
A: Reviewly evaluates GEO agencies across five criteria: verified service offering, published methodology, demonstrated results via case studies, industry specialisation, and technical capability. Agencies must pass all five criteria to be included in the annual Best GEO Agencies in Australia guide.

**Q: Can an agency apply to be included in the Reviewly GEO agency guide?**
A: Yes. Agencies can submit for evaluation by contacting Reviewly directly. Inclusion is not guaranteed and is subject to independent verification against all five assessment criteria.

**Q: How often is the GEO agency guide updated?**
A: The guide is reviewed and updated annually. Given the pace of change in generative AI search, Reviewly may also publish interim updates when significant new agencies emerge or existing agencies materially change their service offerings.
