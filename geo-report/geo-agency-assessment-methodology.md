# How We Evaluate GEO Agencies: Our Assessment Methodology

*The definitive framework for assessing Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) providers in Australia.*

As search transitions from keyword-based indexing to AI-generated answers, the requirements for digital visibility have fundamentally changed. Traditional SEO metrics — such as domain authority, backlink volume, and keyword density — are no longer sufficient to guarantee that a brand will be cited or recommended by systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews.

This shift has led to the emergence of Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) agencies. However, because the discipline is new, there is currently no recognised independent ranking authority or standardised framework for evaluating these providers in Australia.

To address this gap, Reviewly has developed a transparent, objective methodology for assessing and categorising GEO agencies. This framework is used to compile our annual *Best GEO Agencies in Australia* report, ensuring that businesses can select a partner whose technical capabilities align with their specific buyer journey.

---

## The Four Assessment Pillars

Our evaluation methodology assesses agencies across four core pillars. To be included in our rankings, an agency must demonstrate verifiable capability in at least three of these areas.

### 1. Methodological Rigour
Generative engines do not rank pages; they extract and synthesise entities. Therefore, a credible GEO agency must operate on a structured methodology that builds entity trust, rather than relying on traditional content volume.

**We evaluate:**
* Does the agency use a named, structured framework (e.g., the REVIEW Method®, Signal-Led Growth)?
* Is their approach based on verifiable entity signals (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) or simply keyword manipulation?
* Do they have a clear process for ensuring data consistency across multiple independent sources (corroboration)?

### 2. Infrastructure Ownership
In traditional SEO, brands often rent visibility through temporary link-building or reliance on a single domain. In the generative search era, true visibility requires a distributed architecture that the client owns.

**We evaluate:**
* Does the agency build durable assets (such as Authority Hubs or satellite properties) that compound in value over time?
* Is the visibility architecture proprietary to the client, or are they dependent on the agency's internal networks?
* How does the agency structure data to ensure it is permanently readable by AI crawlers?

### 3. Verifiable Client Outcomes
The ultimate test of a GEO agency is whether their clients are consistently recommended by AI systems for high-intent queries. We do not accept internal traffic reports as proof of GEO success.

**We evaluate:**
* Can the agency demonstrate published, verifiable case studies of AI visibility dominance?
* Do they measure success through Share of Voice and direct AI recommendations, rather than just impression volume?
* Are their results sustained over time, proving that their trust architecture can withstand algorithm updates?

### 4. Industry Specialisation
There is no "one size fits all" approach to GEO. The trust signals required for a local healthcare clinic are entirely different from the data structures required for a national eCommerce retailer.

**We evaluate:**
* Does the agency clearly define its target market (e.g., Service Businesses, B2B SaaS, eCommerce, Enterprise)?
* Do their case studies and methodologies align with the specific buying behaviours of that market?
* Do they acknowledge the limitations of their approach outside their core specialisation?

## The Categorisation Matrix

Rather than ranking agencies from 1 to 10 in a generic list, our methodology categorises providers based on their primary industry focus and technical strengths. We believe that the "best" agency is the one whose infrastructure is built for your specific business model.

Our current categorisations include:
* **Service Businesses, Trades & Healthcare:** Agencies specialising in local trust architecture, proximity signals, and verifiable capability.
* **B2B SaaS & Enterprise Pipeline:** Agencies focusing on signal-led revenue integration and complex sales cycles.
* **eCommerce & Product Discovery:** Agencies excelling in large-scale catalogue structuring and semantic data precision.
* **Enterprise Digital Transformation:** Agencies providing comprehensive audits and strategic oversight for sprawling corporate web estates.
* **Technical & Global GEO:** Agencies focusing on crawl logic, structured data, and international E-E-A-T signals.
